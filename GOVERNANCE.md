# Redpact governance

Redpact is a founder-led open-source project. The founder and current lead
maintainer is [@wo658](https://github.com/wo658). The official repository is
[wo658/redpact](https://github.com/wo658/redpact).

## Decisions and access

The lead maintainer has final responsibility for project direction, accepted
changes, releases, maintainer appointments, and moderation of official community
spaces. Contributors can propose changes and challenge decisions respectfully in
issues and pull requests. Discussion informs decisions; votes and contribution
counts do not automatically confer authority.

The lead maintainer may delegate scoped review, merge, release, or moderation
permissions and revoke them when needed. Repository ownership, official package
accounts, signing credentials, and official domains remain controlled by the
founder or an explicitly designated successor. Changes in leadership or delegated
responsibility should be documented in this file through a reviewed PR.

Maintainers should explain significant decisions and rejection reasons publicly,
except where doing so exposes private or security-sensitive information. Be
respectful, discuss the work, and avoid harassment or disclosure of personal data.
Maintainers may remove abusive content and restrict access to official spaces.
Request a private contact route from the lead maintainer for sensitive concerns;
do not post private details in public issues. No independent appeal body or
guaranteed response time is currently established.

## Code, copyright, and commercial use

Project code is available under [Apache-2.0](LICENSE), except where a file or
component specifies its own terms. Contributors retain their copyrights. The
contribution process does not transfer ownership to the lead maintainer.

Anyone, including the founder, may use the code commercially and operate paid
hosted services under the applicable licenses. Official project governance does
not control independent forks, communities, or competing services. Existing
Apache-2.0 grants cannot be withdrawn merely because project strategy changes.

The founder may operate an official Redpact cloud business. Commercial purchases
and contributions do not automatically grant project governance rights. Official
project decisions remain subject to the responsibilities described above.

customary descriptions of origin and reproduction of notices. Identify forks and
independent services clearly, preserve required attribution, and do not claim
official status or endorsement without permission. Ask the lead maintainer about
branding uses beyond those permitted by the license or applicable law.
